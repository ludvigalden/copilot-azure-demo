---
title: Order a new laptop
---

## Check the replacement cycle

Laptops are replaced every four years, or earlier if the hardware no
longer runs the standard software image. You can check your current
model's age on the asset page linked from the intranet start page. If
your laptop is due within three months, order the replacement now so it
arrives before the old one fails.

## Place the order

1. Open the IT service portal and choose *Hardware* and then *New
   laptop*.
2. Pick one of the three standard models. All three run the same image;
   they differ in screen size and weight.
3. Your manager approves the order, and the laptop arrives within five
   business days with setup instructions in the box.

## Move to the new laptop

Sign in with your work account and follow the first-run wizard; your
files in OneDrive, your browser profile and your email appear
automatically. Applications install themselves from the company portal
over the next hour. Keep the old laptop for two weeks, then hand it in
at the service desk so it can be wiped and reissued.
