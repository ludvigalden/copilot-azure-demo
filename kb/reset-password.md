title: Reset your password
---

## When to use this article

Follow these steps if you have forgotten your password, your password has
expired, or you suspect someone else knows it. The reset works from any
device with a browser; you do not need to be on the company network.

## Reset the password

1. Go to the self-service password reset page and enter your work email
   address.
2. Choose a verification method. The default is a code sent to your
   registered mobile phone.
3. Enter the code, then set a new password. It must be at least 14
   characters and must not match any of your last five passwords.
4. Sign in again on every device where you use the old password. Locked
   mobile phones pick up the change the next time they sync.

## If the reset fails

If you do not receive the verification code, check that your phone number
is registered in your security info. If your account is locked after too
many attempts, wait 30 minutes and try again. Contact the service desk if
the lock does not lift on its own; include the exact error message in
your ticket.
