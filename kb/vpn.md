## When you need the VPN

The VPN is only needed for internal systems that are not published to
the internet, such as file shares and legacy business applications. Email,
chat and this help centre work without it. If a page loads slowly or an
internal tool cannot be reached, connect to the VPN before opening a
ticket.

## Connect

1. Open the VPN client from the system tray or menu bar.
2. Select the *Company* profile and click *Connect*.
3. Approve the multi-factor notification on your phone.
4. The icon turns green when the tunnel is up. Internal systems are now
   reachable by their normal addresses.

## Common problems

*Certificate expired*: quit the client completely and start it again; it
downloads a fresh certificate on launch. *Connected but nothing loads*:
disconnect, wait ten seconds, and reconnect; a stale DNS entry is the
usual cause. If the client reports a broken tunnel twice in a row,
restart the machine before contacting the service desk.
