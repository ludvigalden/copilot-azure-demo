---
title: Set up multi-factor authentication
---

## Before you start

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all work accounts. You
need your laptop and your mobile phone. Install an authenticator app from
your phone's app store before you begin; any app that supports push
notifications or time-based codes works.

## Register the authenticator app

1. Sign in to the security info page with your work account.
2. Choose *Add sign-in method* and pick *Authenticator app*.
3. Scan the QR code with the authenticator app, then approve the test
   notification that follows.
4. Keep the app installed. Sign-ins from a new device or location will
   ask you to approve a notification or enter a code.

## Add a backup method

Register at least one backup method, such as a phone number that can
receive SMS codes. If you change or lose your phone, add the new device
as soon as you can: an account with no reachable MFA method can only be
recovered by the service desk, which takes at least one business day.
