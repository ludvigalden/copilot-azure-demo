---
title: Add or fix a printer
---

## Add a printer

1. Open the printer settings on your computer and choose *Add printer*.
2. Office printers are named after the room they stand in, for example
   `PRT-3F12` on floor 3. Pick the printer closest to your desk.
3. The driver installs automatically. Print a test page from the
   printer's properties to confirm.

## The printer does not respond

First check the printer itself: it should show *Ready* on its display.
If it shows an error, clear the paper path and open then close the paper
tray; most jams and offline states clear this way.

If the printer is ready but your jobs stay queued, remove the printer
from your settings, add it again, and resend the job. Old queued jobs
survive reinstallation, so cancel them from the queue window afterwards.

## Badge printing is separate

The printers at the reception desks are managed by facilities, not by
IT. For badge or visitor labels, contact the receptionist on duty; a
support ticket cannot reach those printers.
